# PAIR Research References

This bibliography is intentionally conservative. It includes sources that are either official institutional publications or publications with a verified publisher, DOI, or repository record.

These references inform the conceptual development of **PAIR — Physical AI Readiness**. They should not be interpreted as independent validation of the PAIR methodology.

## Reference Management Note

Before adding a new citation to the core PAIR framework, verify it against at least one of the following:

1. the official institutional publisher;
2. the journal or book publisher;
3. a DOI resolver and publisher landing page;
4. an authoritative government or university repository.

Unverified or secondary citations should not be added to the core methodology bibliography.
